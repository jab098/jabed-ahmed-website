Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

This site is the personal website of Jabed Ahmed, an independent data and analytics consultant based in London. This policy explains what limited information is collected when you visit or get in touch, and how it is used.

Who is responsible

Jabed Ahmed is the data controller for this website. If you have any questions about this policy, contact consulting@jabed.co.uk.

What is collected

  • Contact details you send. If you email or book a call, the information you provide (such as your name, email address and message) is used only to reply and to discuss potential work.

How it is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, provide requested services and maintain the site. It is never sold. It is not shared with third parties except where needed to run the site (for example hosting or scheduling tools) or where required by law.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies needed for it to function and for basic, privacy-respecting analytics. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete any personal information held about you, or ask that it no longer be used. To make a request, email consulting@jabed.co.uk and it will be handled promptly.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with a revised date above.
